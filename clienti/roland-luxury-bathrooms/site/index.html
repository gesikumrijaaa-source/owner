Questions we're often asked

Can't see yours? Send us a WhatsApp or call 07455 504999.

How much does a new bathroom cost?

Every bathroom is different. Size, layout, finishes and what's behind the walls all change the price. That's why we survey first, for free, and then give you a written quote.

Is the survey really free?

Yes. We visit, measure and talk through ideas at no cost and with no obligation to go ahead.

Do you handle the plumbing and electrics?

Yes. Plumbing, electrics, tiling, plastering and fitting are all part of the job, so you don't need to find separate trades.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based at North London Business Park and take on bathrooms across the whole of England.

How long will my bathroom take?

It depends on the size of the room and the scope of the work. We'll give you a timeline with your quote so you can plan around it.

Can you help me choose a design?

Yes. We'll suggest layouts, materials and fittings that suit the room and your budget. You make the final choices.

Do you take away the old bathroom?

Yes. We strip out the old suite, tiles and flooring and take the waste away.
